Learn for the Zurich citizenship test.

Talk to Claude or ChatGPT, or practise right here. You learn the 350 official questions of the canton, one at a time, in your language, and every answer is checked.

Start learning in your browser

How it works

  1. 1

    Add Swiss Passport to Claude or ChatGPT. It takes a minute and needs no account.

  2. 2

    Say which language you want to learn in: German, English, French, Italian, Russian or Ukrainian.

  3. 3

    Do one short lesson a day. When a topic is due again, the tutor asks you first.

Method

One question at a time
A short explanation, then a question right away. The next question only appears after you answer.
Repeat until correct
A question you get wrong comes back at the end of the lesson until you get it right.
Pitfalls explained
Every answer says why it is right and why the tempting wrong answer is wrong.
German included
You learn in your language, and always see the German wording used in the real test.
Mock exam
50 random questions without help, like the canton's official practice test.

Reviews

Every topic comes back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days. A mistake moves it back one step, so you practise what you forget and skip what you know.

Start

In your browser

Learn online, nothing to install. Your progress is saved under a learner code that also works in Claude and ChatGPT.

Claude and ChatGPT

Use this address as a custom connector:

https://swiss-passport-zh.vercel.app/mcp

In Claude

Settings, Connectors, Add custom connector. Paste the address, no login needed. Works on the web, on desktop and in the mobile app, also with voice.

In ChatGPT

Settings, Apps & Connectors, Advanced: turn on Developer mode. Then Create, paste the address, choose no authentication, and enable the app in a chat.

Your progress

On first use you get a learner code like BERG-7K2Q. Write it down and tell it to the tutor in a new chat to continue. No account, no personal data.

Not an official service of the Canton of Zurich. The questions are published by the canton's Gemeindeamt; the explanations and translations are part of this project.
